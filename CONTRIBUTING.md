# Contributing to Agentics Foundation community-projects

Thank you for contributing. This repository follows the Open Source Committee
Charter (`docs/operating-guidelines/committee-charter.md`), Section 11.

## Ordinary contributions use the DCO (not a CLA)

We use the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) 1.1** for ordinary code and docs
contributions. The DCO is lightweight and **does not assign copyright** — you keep
ownership of your work; you simply certify you have the right to contribute it under
the repository's open source license.

To sign off, add a `Signed-off-by` trailer to every commit:

    git commit -s -m "your message"

which appends:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>

Your name and email must be real (no anonymous or pseudonymous sign-offs). By signing
off you agree to the DCO 1.1, reproduced in `DCO.md`.

## Donating an entire project is different

Contributing a patch is covered by the DCO above. **Donating a whole project** to the
Foundation (transferring the repository, trademark, or maintainer obligations) is a
higher-stakes act that requires a separate signed agreement and Board/Legal review. See
the charter Section 11 and the donation-intake process. Open a submission first; the
committee will route it.

## Scope and licensing

- Submitted and supported projects must be open source under a clear, declared license.
- Contributions with unclear licensing or possible IP infringement are escalated, not merged.
